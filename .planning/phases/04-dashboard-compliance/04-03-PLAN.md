<objective>
Build legal compliance pages and data management: Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, AI disclosure, CCPA deletion process, and data retention enforcement.

Purpose: Legal compliance is a launch gate. Privacy Policy and ToS must exist before collecting user data. CCPA deletion is a legal requirement with a 72-hour SLA.
Output: Published legal pages and working data deletion/retention processes.
</objective>

  <files>apps/web/app/privacy/page.tsx, apps/web/app/terms/page.tsx, apps/web/app/about/page.tsx</files>
  <action>
1. Privacy Policy page (/privacy) covering (LGAL-01):
   - Data collection: what data, why, how long retained
   - Tier classification: Tier 1 (encrypted PII), Tier 2 (operational), Tier 3 (public)
   - AI disclosure: how AI is used, what models, what data sent to Anthropic
   - CCPA rights: right to know, delete, opt-out
   - Contact information for privacy requests
   
2. Terms of Service page (/terms) covering (LGAL-02):
   - "Not legal advice" disclaimer prominent
   - Lobbying disclaimer
   - User-as-author assertion (user is the author of their civic issue)
   - Service description and limitations
   - Content policy (moderation rules)
   - Payment terms and refund policy
   
3. About page (/about):
   - AI disclosure: transparent about AI-generated letters
   - CAN-SPAM compliance statement
   - Physical address (required by CAN-SPAM)

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: Build CCPA deletion endpoint</name>
  <files>apps/api/src/routes/compliance.ts, apps/api/src/index.ts</files>
  <action>
Create apps/api/src/routes/compliance.ts:
- POST /api/compliance/delete-my-data — CCPA right-to-deletion (LGAL-04):
  - Requires auth
  - Soft-deletes all user data (submissions, campaigns, letters)
  - Cryptographic erasure: rotates encryption key version for user's Tier 1 fields
  - 72-hour SLA logged to audit trail
  - Returns confirmation
  
- GET /api/compliance/data-export — CCPA right-to-know:
  - Returns all user data in JSON format
  - Decrypts Tier 1 fields for export

Register in index.ts.
  </action>
</task>
