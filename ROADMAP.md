# CivicState Roadmap

## Overview

CivicState's existing roadmap describes a four-phase build from foundation through dashboard and compliance, with all planned phases marked complete on 2026-04-25 [evidence: .planning/ROADMAP.md]. The business plan reframes the next work as validation, because the repo contains implementation and planning artifacts but no production traction proof [evidence: BUSINESS.md].

## Existing Narrative Preserved

The original roadmap sequence remains directionally right: infrastructure, AI pipeline, payment and delivery, then dashboard and compliance [evidence: .planning/ROADMAP.md]. The repo also preserves the same core value: AI-powered regulation research, verified legal citations, official targeting by ZIP code, and one-click transactional delivery priced from $5 to $25 [evidence: .planning/PROJECT.md].

## Current Reality

Treat the product as built-but-unvalidated. `.planning/ROADMAP.md` marks phases complete, while `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` still lists many launch requirements unchecked and `.planning/STATE.md` says only Foundation was complete as of 2026-04-25 [evidence: .planning/ROADMAP.md; .planning/REQUIREMENTS.md; .planning/STATE.md].

## Buildable Shape

- [ ] **Thesis Current:** reconcile roadmap status against actual tests and running services in a single validation checklist.
- [ ] **Customer Definition:** run one bounded metro beta with a manually reviewed issue category and real Stripe payment.
- [ ] **Product And Build Reality:** verify one end-to-end submission from wizard through preview, payment, delivery queue, and dashboard.
- [ ] **Market Sizing:** capture source, visitor, preview, payment, refund, and repeat metrics needed to replace assumptions.
- [ ] **Revenue Model:** confirm $5, $15, and $25 package economics [evidence: .planning/PROJECT.md] with actual token, email, Stripe, refund, and support costs.
- [ ] **Go To Market:** publish only opted-in campaign pages from successful beta jobs and measure organic impressions.
- [ ] **Risks And Anti-Plan:** stress-test citation failure, official bounce, legal-adjacent content, and operator review backlog before expansion.

## Validation Gates

The product should not expand beyond the first beta until paid conversion, delivery success, citation verification, refund rate, and operator load are measured. The earlier planning target names 3% paid conversion, 85% inbox placement, 95% federal/state official coverage, 60% local official coverage, and chargebacks below 0.5% [evidence: .planning/PROJECT.md].

## Deferred Until Validation

Keep subscriptions, public comments, votes, coalition mechanics, certified mail, fax, multilingual support, third-party API access, crowdfunding adjacency, and national rollout deferred until the transactional loop works with real users [evidence: .planning/PROJECT.md; .planning/GENESIS.md; MASTER_PLAN.md].

## Evidence And Date Hygiene

Update this roadmap after each validation milestone. The current upgrade is dated 2026-06-21 [evidence: dispatch current_date], while most source planning material is dated 2026-04-25 or March 2026 [evidence: .planning/PROJECT.md; MASTER_PLAN.md].
